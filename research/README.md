# PETRA Research Index

This directory is the scientific memory of the project. The rule is simple: **if the engine presents a behavior as biology, the scientific basis, context, approximation and provenance belong here.**

## Research structure

- `PRIMARY_SOURCE_AUDIT.md` — directly checked papers for the flagship *E. coli* + ciprofloxacin scenario.
- `CLAIM_LEDGER.md` — what PETRA may claim, and the evidence tier of each claim.
- `growth_and_resources.md` — nutrient-limited growth and spatial colony biology.
- `antibiotic_pharmacodynamics.md` — concentration-dependent drug effect.
- `mutation_and_resistance.md` — Luria–Delbrück logic, resistance states, fitness costs and compensation.
- `spatial_evolution.md` — spatial gradients and the MEGA-plate precedent.
- `stochastic_simulation.md` — Gillespie/tau-leap rationale and cohort modeling.
- `phage_extension.md` — scientifically defensible lytic-phage extension.
- `machine_learning.md` — where ML belongs and where it does not.
- `REFERENCES.md` — working bibliography.

## Evidence tiers

- **A:** direct experimental measurement / validated submodel for the stated context.
- **B:** established biological mechanism with a model abstraction or parameter transfer.
- **C:** engineering approximation needed for tractability.
- **D:** purely visual/game abstraction; must never be presented as a biological law.

## Core principle

Missing evidence is not permission to invent a number. Parameters that are not yet grounded remain explicitly unset until a source or calibration decision is made.
