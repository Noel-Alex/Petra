# MG1655 chloramphenicol environment binding — Greulich MOPS contexts

Issue: #928  
Drug authority owner: #930  
Physical spatial calibration follow-on: #938 / #657  
Product compatibility owner: #879

## Decision

Petra may implement and validate the Greulich et al. 2015 chloramphenicol **numerical response family** for *E. coli* K-12 MG1655, but the current spatial flagship cannot truthfully choose the glucose or glycerol family from its dimensionless `model-resource` field.

The source model is conditioned on the **drug-free exponential growth rate** `lambda0` measured for a defined nutrient environment. In Greulich et al., nutrient quality is varied by changing defined MOPS media and supplementation; `lambda0` is the resulting pre-antibiotic exponential growth rate. It is not a concentration unit, not a Monod half-saturation constant, and not an instruction to reinterpret an arbitrary local resource scalar as glucose or glycerol.

The environment-binding question is therefore resolved as follows:

- **isolated/source-context chloramphenicol curve validation is supported now** through #930;
- **spatial product enablement is not identifiable from the current flagship ecology**;
- the first concrete promotion path is a distinct, versioned **MG1655 MOPS-glucose physical/calibrated environment bridge** under #938 / #657;
- the existing `model-resource` flagship remains unchanged and cannot select a Greulich medium family.

Research is OFF for this mapping decision. Research/calibration remains ON only for #938's missing physical resource/transport/growth bridge.

## Source context

Primary response source:

Greulich P, Scott M, Evans MR, Allen RJ. “Growth-dependent bacterial susceptibility to ribosome-targeting antibiotics.” *Molecular Systems Biology* 11(3):796 (2015). DOI `10.15252/MSB.20145949`.

Defined-medium source referenced by Greulich:

Neidhardt FC, Bloch PL, Smith DF. “Culture Medium for Enterobacteria.” *Journal of Bacteriology* 119(3):736–747 (1974). DOI `10.1128/jb.119.3.736-747.1974`.

Greulich experimental envelope relevant to the first chloramphenicol authority:

- organism/background: *E. coli* K-12 MG1655;
- temperature: 37 °C;
- medium family: modified Neidhardt potassium-MOPS defined medium;
- base carbon-source families:
  - glucose 0.2% w/v;
  - glycerol 0.2% v/v;
- intermediate/faster drug-free growth conditions were generated by adding casamino acids or nucleotides + all amino acids to the corresponding base medium;
- cultures were liquid batch cultures in shaken tubes, not a spatial agar-plate transport experiment;
- antibiotic response is parameterized by the measured drug-free exponential growth rate `lambda0` of the nutrient condition.

The chloramphenicol fitted parameter pairs remain separate by carbon-source family:

| family | lambda0_star (h^-1) | IC50_star (uM) |
|---|---:|---:|
| glycerol | 1.83 ± 0.06 | 2.49 ± 0.05 |
| glucose | 1.28 ± 0.02 | 4.50 ± 0.05 |

These fitted scales do **not** remove the need for a valid `lambda0` input.

## Exact role of lambda0

For Petra, `lambda0` means:

> the drug-free exponential growth rate, in h^-1, for the exact source-compatible physiological/environment context whose chloramphenicol response is being evaluated.

It does not mean:

- local resource concentration;
- the current arbitrary `model-resource` value;
- a generic strain maximum growth rate independent of medium;
- instantaneous net growth after drug/death/capacity effects;
- a renderer-observed colony expansion rate.

Greulich's physiology model assumes a pre-drug growth state associated with the nutrient environment. Feeding an instantaneous spatial ecology rate into the cubic would be a **new transfer assumption**: Petra would be asserting that locally varying, possibly resource-depleted/crowded cells have the same adapted physiological mapping as the well-mixed exponential cultures. This pack does not authorize that transfer.

## Why the current flagship cannot be rebound by naming

Current Petra ecology uses:

- dimensionless `model-resource`;
- `model-biomass`;
- an engineering execution profile;
- no physical glucose concentration mapping;
- no source-owned glucose diffusion/uptake law;
- no physical biomass yield bridge;
- no validated mapping from local resource/crowding state to source-compatible drug-free `lambda0`.

Therefore all of these shortcuts are forbidden:

```text
model-resource = glucose
resource == 1.0 => 0.2% glucose
local Monod-like division rate => Greulich lambda0
flagship maxDivisionRate => source MOPS lambda0
same 37 C label => same physiological context
```

They would change the scientific claim without evidence.

## Liquid MOPS versus the spatial dish

Greulich's quantitative response data are from adapted, shaken liquid cultures. Petra's visual product is a spatial dish with local fields and crowding. The antibiotic **cellular response model** can be source-backed while the **environment bridge** remains unbound.

A spatial MOPS scenario needs a separate validation argument for, at minimum:

1. physical carbon-source field units;
2. transport/diffusion appropriate to the chosen product geometry;
3. resource uptake/consumption;
4. biomass/resource yield or another explicit conservation bridge;
5. mapping from the drug-free local ecology state to `lambda0`;
6. how oxygen/aeration/crowding assumptions differ from the source liquid cultures;
7. zero-drug validation before chloramphenicol is applied.

The Greulich inhibition curves cannot identify those quantities by themselves. Fitting them only to antibiotic response would be circular and underdetermined.

## First promotion target: MOPS-glucose

For project scope, #938 starts with the **glucose family only**:

- modified Neidhardt potassium-MOPS;
- glucose 0.2% w/v base condition;
- MG1655;
- 37 °C;
- exact glucose-family Greulich fit from #930.

This is an engineering scope choice, not a claim that glucose is universally preferable. Glycerol remains a distinct future context and must not be averaged or substituted.

The reason to separate them is scientific identity: Greulich reports distinct glucose/glycerol susceptibility trends and fits.

## Calibration contract for #938 / #657

A valid spatial promotion must use the existing bounded calibration architecture rather than hidden hand-tuning.

Required:

- versioned objective and dataset/target identity;
- explicit parameter bounds from evidence or clearly labelled engineering ranges;
- fit targets separated from hold-out validation targets;
- zero-drug environmental validation before any antibiotic objective;
- sensitivity/identifiability report;
- multiple acceptable solutions reported when present;
- calibrated values never relabelled measured;
- no parameter chosen solely because it makes the chloramphenicol curve look right.

The local experiment is pre-registered as `chloramphenicol-mops-resource-calibration`, but remains blocked until #938 has an honest target/bounds/forward-model contract.
