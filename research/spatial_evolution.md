# Spatial Evolution

Spatial structure is not cosmetic. It changes access to resources, drug concentration, neighbors and which lineage reaches an advancing front.

## MEGA-plate precedent

Baym et al. (2016), *Science*, DOI `10.1126/science.aag0822`, provides a powerful visual precedent: *E. coli* populations expanded through regions of increasing antibiotic concentration while distinct lineages and mutations arose.

PETRA may use this as inspiration for a **band/zone mode**:

- inoculum begins in a low/no-drug zone;
- drug concentration changes spatially across bands;
- lineages expand only where local net growth permits;
- mutation and spatial position jointly determine which lineage reaches the next front.

## What PETRA should reproduce qualitatively

- multiple lineages can coexist;
- adaptation is front- and location-dependent;
- the most resistant genotype need not dominate globally;
- a lineage can be blocked by other biomass even if its drug phenotype is favorable.

## What PETRA must not claim without calibration

- exact MEGA-plate times;
- exact colony geometry;
- exact mutation sequence;
- quantitative equivalence to the Baym experiment.

The correct label is **mechanistically inspired / qualitatively validated**, unless experiment-specific geometry and parameters are intentionally reconstructed.
