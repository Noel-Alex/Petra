# Temperature response

Temperature strongly changes microbial growth, but one arbitrary “temperature penalty” is not defensible.

## Suboptimal range
Ratkowsky et al. 1982, DOI `10.1128/jb.149.1.1-5.1982`, established the square-root relationship for many bacterial growth-rate datasets below the optimum:

`sqrt(mu) = b (T - T_min)`.

## Full biokinetic range
The relationship above is not sufficient above the optimum. Ratkowsky et al. 1983, DOI `10.1128/JB.154.3.1222-1226.1983`, extends the model to include minimum/maximum temperature bounds.

## Petra rule
- keep the flagship fixed at its source-relevant reference temperature initially;
- add temperature only with a species/scenario-specific cardinal/extended curve;
- show the validated temperature range;
- do not extrapolate into thermal killing regimes unless the model explicitly includes death/inactivation data.
