# Calm vector visual-system DOX

## Purpose

Own Petra's approved calm, code-feasible vector art direction for presentation surfaces without becoming a source of scientific state.

## Palette authority

- `calmVector.ts` is the canonical presentation palette for the visual reset under #456.
- Surfaces use deep desaturated ink/navy foundations with muted teal/mint, warm amber/coral, olive, cream, and restrained neutral text.
- Do not reintroduce neon cyan/violet as the default product language, bloom-heavy glows, electric spikes, noisy photorealistic textures, or baked illustration backgrounds.
- Critical scientific identity still requires text, shape, pattern, icon, or other non-color redundancy. Palette is never scientific authority.

## Biological primitive authority

- `biologyMotif.ts` owns deterministic presentation-only motif geometry.
- Geometry must remain simple enough for SVG/Canvas/Pixi primitives: ellipses, rounded rods, polylines/paths, flat fills, masks, and restrained gradients.
- Motifs are decorative/explanatory only. Never count motif rods, read motif positions, or infer lineage/resource/drug values from them.
- Stage-specific motifs may explain concepts already supplied by authoritative story/runtime state; they must not fabricate biological events.

## Motion

- Calm-vector adapters consume existing Petra motion policy/tokens. This subtree does not define biological durations or component-local semantic timing.
- Full motion favors continuity, clean grow/fade/transform transitions, and subtle field motion.
- Reduced/Off preserve the same explanatory composition without spatial/decorative movement.
- Avoid constant bouncing and random particle noise.

## Integration

- New App/Pixi surfaces should consume this palette/shape language when their active leases permit rather than inventing parallel aesthetics.
- The live dish remains authoritative-state-driven; visual primitives may aggregate/LOD presentation but cannot feed back into simulation.
